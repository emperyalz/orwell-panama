# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Users
People looking up a politician after seeing them in the news, returning for sourced records and comparisons.
## Product Purpose
A global political profile reference product. Panama is the first working country model, followed by the United States, Canada, and Mexico.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Existing Next.js 16, React, Convex and Vercel application. Preserve the existing directory and source records. Common biographical core with country and office specific records. Separate historical voting data from directory roles. Missing evidence must remain visibly missing. Search visibility is an ambition, not a promised ranking.
## Brand Commitments
Existing ORWELL vector marks remain unchanged. The September 14 approved typographic party draft governs politician profiles: huge condensed names, left portrait, party colors, neutral ruled evidence grid. Earlier Transfermarkt/FotMob research informs information architecture only. Spanish is the existing Panama interface language.
## Evidence on Hand
Production Convex fleet-vole-527; existing public portraits, account links, legislative imports, Espacio Cívico documents. No social post table exists in this application's schema. Birth details, election results and financial coverage vary or are absent. Current office tenure is not explicitly modeled.
## Product Principles
Every significant fact needs provenance. Distinguish salary, office payroll, declared assets and estimated wealth. A stored zero is not automatically evidence of absence. Compare like periods and offices.
## Open decisions
Editorial verification workflow, current-term ingestion and expansion datasets require further work. This preview implements the authorized profile reference direction directly from the supplied brief; it does not establish a standing image-generation workflow preference.
