# Six homepage PNG concepts

Eric requested six flat visual designs on October 8, 2026. These are review images only, not functional pages or a homepage replacement.

1. Political directory: portrait-led browsing, large search, office categories, comparison selection tray.
2. Comparison first: prominent selected profiles and aligned preview rows, discover-and-compare as the central action.
3. Editorial index: newspaper-style hierarchy, large featured portrait, source-backed archive stories, compact directory.
4. Territory first: province and municipality navigation as the entry point, then local people and official records.
5. Career first: career timeline as the central visual, connecting elections, offices, and documented actions.
6. Intelligence first: archive coverage and communication activity, with people and original sources directly linked.

Design references already reviewed: The Index resource library, Mobbin's Zillow comparison flow, and Customer.io analytics filtering and table patterns. These inform interactions and hierarchy, not copied brand assets.

No invented political scores, popularity claims, or evidence-free performance rankings. Native politician portraits retain the approved identity, official pose, lean silhouette, and overlap with the flag. Official brand marks preserve their source geometry and colors.

Generation uses the built-in image tool. Each concept is a separate image request. The current homepage screenshot supplies brand context; local approved portraits supply identity references. Final files: 01.png through 06.png. The inaccurate map draft was replaced with a typographic province index. Prompt specifications are recorded in prompts.json. The intelligence chart is explicitly illustrative, not live data.
