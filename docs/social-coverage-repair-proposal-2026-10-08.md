## Operational guardrails

Monitor currently applies different pause behavior in different paths: the per-platform due queries check both `isActive` and `!isPaused`, while `convex/scraping/worker.ts:getAccountsDue` checks `isActive` but does not check `isPaused`. All seven existing rows in this batch are inactive, so that discrepancy does not change their present status. Before activating any paused account, reconcile the worker path so that `isPaused` is honored consistently.

The current reactivation mutation sets a row’s priority due immediately and clears its consecutive failure count. For cost control, do not reactivate as a side effect of changing an identity label. For Ernesto, ownership is now verified, but polling should remain a separately reviewed operational action. For Jorge’s accepted professional page, a new row would need a normal, non-immediate first poll and no inherited account history.

Keep identity, profile media, and post provenance as separate fields. Verified ownership establishes who controls a profile; it does not by itself prove that a cached avatar is current, that a stored image belongs in the official headshot slot, or that every historical post was correctly attributed. No avatar or post reassignment is proposed here.
