# Social identity audit A, 8 October 2026

The first 30 politicians alphabetically have 70 stored accounts. 5 accounts meet the ownership rule; 65 remain unresolved. No production records changed.

Verified requires an official party/institution or owner-controlled source that links the exact account. Candidate-submitted Espacio Cívico records are useful corroboration, but are not sufficient by themselves. A matching name, bio, role/location, stored CONFIRMED flag, badge, or avatar alone does not prove account control. Every stored profile URL was fetched; X pages exposed profile metadata, while most Instagram/TikTok/Facebook fetches returned generic shells or access errors.

## Material findings

- Aixa Santamaría’s Instagram is verified by the Peruvian Embassy’s official report, which names the Chiriquí governor and exact `@aixa.santamaria` account. The 2024 candidate record repeats that exact URL.
- VAMOS’s official caucus roster links Alexandra Brenes’s current Instagram and X and Eduardo Gaitán’s current Instagram and X. The roster ties each person to circuit 8-2; the X profile descriptions are consistent.
- Augusto Palacios: VAMOS currently links @tutopalacios05, while inventory has @tutopalaciosm. The stored X profile is live and describes circuit 4-1. Account/rename continuity is unproven. Espacio Cívico also surfaces an unlisted Facebook URL; hold both changes pending reconciliation.
- Ariel Vallarino: the candidate page links @antonioalexopulos, not the inventoried @arielvallarino. This conflict does not prove the stored account is wrong. Keep it unresolved.
- Eduardo Gaitán’s candidate record links TikTok @washpanama; the public page describes floor-polishing and epoxy flooring services. It may be a related business, but should not be represented as a verified political account.
- Cirilo Salas’s X profile description references “el ejército de Chávez”; his party role is confirmed by news coverage, but the official party page does not link this profile. Treat as a possible namesake or stale assignment, not a proven wrong account.
- Crispiano Adames has conflicting circuit information: his live X bio says 8-3; the current PRD deputy roster says 8-7. The profile and political record need reconciliation.
- Eliécer Castrellón’s X profile describes a mayor of Tolé, while the inventory assigns him to a deputy profile. A prior role or namesake is possible; no ownership link was found.
- Carlos Afú and Gertrudis Rodríguez have no stored social accounts. Searches using their names, Panama, role, and circuit plus the available official/candidate pages did not locate an owner-linked account. This does not prove they have no public accounts.
