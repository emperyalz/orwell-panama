## Acceptance rules

- Exact account link from a primary political/official source with matching politician identity and office/circuit.
- Owner-controlled hubs require corroboration from an established political account or matching platform biography.
- Names, badges, avatars, follower counts and search snippets alone do not establish ownership.
- Private/personal and professional accounts may both belong to a politician. A preferred professional account does not make the previous personal account an impersonator.
- An inaccessible account is unresolved, not automatically wrong.
